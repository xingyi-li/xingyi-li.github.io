The idea

Build worlds.
Carry experience forward.

Moving a scene generator to a new engine requires more than exporting its output. Its planning, tools, and stored experience may depend on the original backend.

SceneAgent separates persistent scene state, task-local reasoning, and reusable skills. Coordinated agents construct the scene; backend adapters handle how it is realized in each engine.

Persistent worlds. Fresh agents.

Separate what the world remembers
from what each agent needs to reason about.

01 / COORDINATE

Plan, then construct

The Orchestrator retrieves skills. The Planner organizes semantic subtasks. Fresh Builders query the persistent scene state and carry out each task.

02 / REALIZE

Isolate the backend

A canonical SceneContext stores the evolving world. Adapters handle coordinate conventions, asset bindings, and export to the target engine.

03 / REUSE

Retain approved experience

Human-approved traces become asset-ID-free Layout skills and semantic Content skills, ready to retrieve and recombine in later generations.
